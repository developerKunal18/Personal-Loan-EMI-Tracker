1. Features:
Add and manage multiple loans.
Calculate monthly EMI automatically.
View loan details and payment schedules.
Record monthly EMI payments.
Track paid and pending EMIs.
Calculate outstanding loan balance.
View complete payment history.
Delete loan records.
